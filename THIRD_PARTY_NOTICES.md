# Source attribution

The mass-based ADM1 equation lineage includes the work of Sören Weinrich and
Michael Nelles, including *Systematic simplification of the Anaerobic Digestion
Model No. 1 (ADM1) — Model development and stoichiometric analysis*,
[doi:10.1016/j.biortech.2021.125124](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biortech.2021.125124).
The upstream MIT notice is retained in `third_party_licenses/Weinrich-MIT.txt`.
The local Python equation ports, their prior modifications and archived inputs
are recorded in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`; no original-author endorsement is implied.

The effective grass-silage/cattle-slurry inputs cite the earlier project's
Tisocco 2024 / Weinrich-derived characterization in their source-note columns.
Read those columns and the reconstruction record before reusing them.
Publisher articles, original figures and third-party source PDFs are not bundled.

The new adapters, reconstructed analysis modules and documentation are released
under the repository MIT license. Third-party notices remain applicable.
